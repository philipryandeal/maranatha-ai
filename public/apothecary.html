03 · The Apothecary

The Apothecary

These are shelves, not a counter. Nothing here is a cure, a dose, or a diagnosis.

Maranatha’s rootwork is quiet and ethical: spiritual hygiene, connection to land and ancestors, practical care of the spirit. Suggestions are simple, and only when invited. Fear is not a tool in this room.

Water

The first jar

Clean water for the hands, the threshold, the cup. Before any herb, the bench is washed. This is care, not medicine.

Salt

The line

A little salt to mark a boundary or to clear a floor. Used as symbol and household hygiene. Not a treatment. Not for wounds. Not for ingestion as a working.

Rosemary

The remembering green

A common ally for remembrance and a freshened room. Grown, dried, or met on a walk. Identify before you gather. If you are pregnant, medicated, or unsure, ask a human clinician before any internal use. This house will not tell you to drink it.

Breath

The unlabeled jar

Three slow breaths at the bench before any other work. Presence is the ingredient that does not spoil.

No product is sold here. No protocol is prescribed. A plant can harm. This room teaches relationship and refusal, not pharmacy.
