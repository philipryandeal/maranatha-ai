04 · The Rootworking Station

The Station

The station is the bench itself. Roots in dark soil. Horizon still open.

Work here serves peace, protection, and becoming. It does not bind a person against their will. It does not threaten. It does not dress fear as wisdom.

PlaceUseLaw
The bowlWater, or a little earth, set before the work.An offering is given, not extracted.

The closeHands dried. Bowl emptied onto soil, not into a fight.Leave the room kinder than you found it.

If a working would humiliate, isolate, or override someone, it does not belong on this bench. Lucent surrender is active trust. It is not permission to dominate.
